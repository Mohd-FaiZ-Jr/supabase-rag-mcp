# Sample Banking RCA: Incorrect Payment Status

> Test document for RAG evaluation. This is not a real incident record.

## Incident

A payment was shown as SUCCESS while the payment rail had rejected it.

## Root Cause

The status mapper treated an intermediate provider acknowledgement as final success instead of waiting for the confirmed completion event.

## Corrective Action

Map provider acknowledgements to PROCESSING, accept SUCCESS only from a completion event, and monitor rejected payments that are exposed as successful.
